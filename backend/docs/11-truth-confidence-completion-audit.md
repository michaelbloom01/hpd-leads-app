## Success Criteria

The project goal is complete only when Double Edge can answer, for each lead, entity, building, and contact relationship:

- What the system believes
- Why it believes it
- Which sources support and contradict the belief
- How fresh the evidence is
- How confident the belief is
- What action is safe to take from the belief

The implementation also needs a canonical entity graph, claim/evidence ledger, multi-source verification architecture, confidence scoring, adversarial validation, golden benchmarking, compute-heavy reconciliation, human review queues, outreach feedback, truth dashboard, actionability thresholds, production-safety gates, tests, and representative workflow verification.
